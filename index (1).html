Privacy Policy for Lumen Merge

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Developer: Shoaib · Contact: contact.youplix@gmail.com

1. Summary

Lumen Merge is a single-player puzzle game. It does not require an account, does not have a server of its own, and does not collect personal information from you. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device only (browser local storage): best score, highest orb, coin balance, settings (music, sound, vibration, accessibility options), unlocked skins and themes, statistics, codex and achievement progress, daily missions progress, the daily gift timer, and your unfinished game so it can resume. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Advertising

The game may show advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect or access device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, app interaction data, and ad performance data in order to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). For details on how Google uses data, see the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you may be asked for your consent to personalised ads through a consent message before ads are shown.

4. In-app purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, they are processed entirely by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store your payment information.

5. Children

Lumen Merge is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising shown in the game may be restricted for children under applicable rules.

6. Security

Because the game stores data only on your device, the security of that data depends on your device. We recommend keeping your device locked and updated.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact contact.youplix@gmail.com.
